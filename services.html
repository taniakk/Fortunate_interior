How We Work

A clear process,
from first call to handover.

No jargon. No overwhelming catalogues. Four steps from brief to installation.

01

Consultation

We visit your space, understand your routine, and take detailed measurements. Free of charge.

02

Design & Quote

We present layouts, 3D views, and material options — along with a clear, itemised quote.

03

Fabrication

Our in-house carpenters build every unit. Weekly progress updates keep you informed.

04

Installation

We install, finish, and hand over. Then we stay on call for a full year for any fixes.
