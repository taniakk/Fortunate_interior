Our Process

How we turn a brief
into a home you'll live in
for the next decade.

Seven steps, one team, no surprises. Every project follows the same rhythm — from the first site visit to the day you move in.

01
Duration · 1–2 days

Site Visit & Consultation

We start at your space, not at a catalogue. One of our designers visits your home or office, listens to what you actually want, and takes detailed measurements. We'll ask about how you cook, where you work, and what frustrates you about the current layout.

What happens

  • ✓ Full property walkthrough with measurements
  • ✓ Discussion on your brief, budget range, and timelines
  • ✓ Notes on site constraints — light, plumbing, wiring, walls
  • ✓ Photos of the existing space for reference

Free of charge. No obligation to proceed.

02
Duration · 3–5 days

Design Brief & Concept Direction

Back at the studio, we sketch out layout options and pin down a design direction. You'll see hand-drawn plans, mood references, and two or three different layout approaches for the same room — so you can choose what actually suits your family.

What happens

  • ✓ Hand-drawn or CAD layout options
  • ✓ Material and colour direction
  • ✓ Mood board with references
  • ✓ Preliminary budget ballpark
03
Duration · 5–7 days

3D Views & Detailed Quote

Before a single piece of plywood is cut, you see 3D views of the space. You'll know exactly what your kitchen or wardrobe will look like — down to the hardware finish. Alongside, you get a detailed itemised quote. No hidden costs.

What happens

  • ✓ 3D renders of every room
  • ✓ Itemised quote with line-level pricing
  • ✓ Material specifications listed clearly
  • ✓ Timeline and payment schedule
04
Duration · 1–2 days

Material Selection at the Studio

We don't pick materials from a screen. You visit our Jogeshwari West studio, touch real plywood samples, see how laminates sit under natural light, and feel how hinges open. This one visit usually settles 90% of the design decisions.

What happens

  • ✓ Plywood, laminate, acrylic, and veneer samples
  • ✓ Hardware options — hinges, channels, handles
  • ✓ Stone and countertop selection
  • ✓ Final price confirmation
05
Duration · 3–5 weeks

In-House Fabrication

Every unit — every cabinet, every drawer, every wardrobe panel — is built in our own workshop by carpenters who've been with us for years. You get weekly progress updates, with photos of your actual project as it comes together.

What happens

  • ✓ Weekly progress updates with photos
  • ✓ Quality checks at each stage of fabrication
  • ✓ Single point of contact for all questions
  • ✓ No subcontracting — one team, one standard
06
Duration · 1–2 weeks

Installation & On-Site Finishing

The same team that fabricated your units installs them. This is where details matter most — alignment, joint closing, edge banding, and the way a drawer slides. We finish everything on-site and adjust as needed.

What happens

  • ✓ Full installation by the fabrication team
  • ✓ Electrical, plumbing, and hardware final fittings
  • ✓ Cleaning and site handover preparation
  • ✓ Detailed walkthrough with you
07
Duration · Ongoing

Handover & One-Year Aftercare

The project doesn't end at handover. We stay on call for a full year — for hinge adjustments, minor fixes, or anything that comes up as you start using the space. If something needs attention, we come back.

What happens

  • ✓ Final walkthrough and sign-off
  • ✓ Care and maintenance guidance for your materials
  • ✓ One year of free aftercare service
  • ✓ Priority scheduling for any future work
