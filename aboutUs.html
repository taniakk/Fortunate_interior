Manifesto
A Note From The Studio

On slowness,
and why it's a feature.

Most interior firms brag about speed. We'd rather brag about the drawer that still glides smoothly on year seven. The wardrobe that hasn't sagged. The kitchen counter that's seen thousands of dosas and still looks like day one.

That kind of longevity doesn't come from shortcuts. It comes from picking the right ply, using the right adhesive, and having the same carpenter do the job he's done for fifteen years. It comes from saying no to materials that look great on a mood board but won't survive Mumbai's monsoon.

We're a small studio by choice. It means we can take on fewer projects, spend more hours per home, and personally walk every client through the details. If that sounds slower than what you're used to — good. That's the point.

What We Believe

Three quiet convictions.

i.

Every home deserves a brief, not a template.

We've never reused a layout. Not once. Every project starts with a blank page and a long conversation about how you actually spend your days.

ii.

The bones matter more than the surface.

We obsess over ply grades, edge banding, and hinge tolerances — the things you'll never see, but will feel every single day for the next twenty years.

iii.

Aftercare is part of the design.

Our relationship doesn't end at handover. We're a phone call away for a full year — and most of our work comes from clients referring their neighbours.

The Journey

Fifteen years,
four turning points.

2010 · The First Project

A 1BHK kitchen, redrawn seven times.

Our first client was a family in Jogeshwari who wanted a modular kitchen on a strict budget. We redrew the plan seven times before we got it right. That kitchen still stands today — and so does the friendship.

2014 · The Workshop Opens

We stopped outsourcing carpentry.

Bringing fabrication in-house changed everything. We could control the quality of every joint, every hinge, every edge band. The lead times got longer. The finish got dramatically better. Clients noticed.

2019 · Full-Service Studio

Design, materials, craft — one address.

We consolidated design, procurement, fabrication, and installation into a single studio. No more coordination headaches for clients. No more finger-pointing between vendors. One team, one accountability.

Today · Still Slower Than We Could Be

A studio that chooses quality over scale.

We could grow faster. We've been asked to. But growth would mean letting go of the things that make our work feel personal — and that's a trade we're not willing to make.
